1
Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome on your Pixel.
2
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app"), then tap Install.

Once added, mono.cast will have its own icon on your Pixel home screen and open in fullscreen without the Chrome address bar.
